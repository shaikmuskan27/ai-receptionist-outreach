Daily checklist

1. **Pick companies** with high call or chat volume (clinics, salons, real estate, hotels, coaching, home services).
2. **Find people in Apollo**: managers or ops heads first, then staff. Save name, role, phone, email.
3. **India:** call and pitch, then email.
4. **Abroad:** LinkedIn connect, then email, then WhatsApp.
5. **Follow up** on days 2, 5 and 10. Stop after 5 touches.
6. **On reply:** ask for a 15-minute call or demo, offer two time slots.
